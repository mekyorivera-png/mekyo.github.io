Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Chinese Master Beginner is a Mandarin study app for Beginner Levels 1 and 2. This policy describes the current guest / local version.

1. Summary

The app runs on your device without an account. Learning progress, favorites, and display preferences stay on the device. This version does not send study data to our servers.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Script preference (Simplified, Traditional, or Both)
  • Sentence-display preferences and theme
  • Flashcard review history and quiz results
  • Favorites, streak, and study-time totals

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or precise location.

3. Information not collected in this version

No cloud sign-in, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, microphone, camera, contacts, or photo-library access.

4. Your choices

Use Profile → Delete local data to erase on-device progress. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

5. Children

This is a general education app with no chat or public user-generated content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: support@chinesemaster.app

The app does not claim official HSK certification.
